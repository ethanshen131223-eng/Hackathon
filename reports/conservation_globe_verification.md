# Conservation globe and hosted release

Implemented a third entry point, `/globe.html`, with rotatable deck.gl Earth, eight geographic shortcuts, directional/zoom controls, keyboard navigation, marine/coastal Protected Planet display boundaries, an accessible filterable list, and selected-source details. The existing ocean mapper now has a local Natural Earth land/ocean background and country/water labels when no Google key is configured. Both views use the same unmodified color tint; the map texture is cropped to Mercator's latitude extent.

Natural Earth I land-cover, shaded relief and water was downloaded from its public-domain raster distribution and resized to 2048×1024. Provenance, hash, image conversion, and historical/cartographic limitations are included in `web/public/globe/provenance.json`. Country label anchors come from Natural Earth v5.1.2; broad water names are manually positioned orientation labels.

Static hosting includes 20 cached FML photos and their original annotations, plus 12 precomputed pollution snapshots with 554 source-supported water tiles and 12 interpolated PNG frames. The hosted case pool and exported status are labeled explicitly; the local backend retains 3,636 eligible photos and dynamic water-area-weighted grids. Hosted tiles retain a fixed 25 km² resolution. Unsupported points do not receive a fabricated density. Conservation boundaries and WoRMS searches remain remote service requests.

Verification: 99 frontend tests pass; Vite builds all three entry points successfully. Tests cover globe camera/bounds, date-line/polar handling, region/filter/selection/toggle UI, stale-request suppression, WebGL/source recovery, hosted case choice and daily stability, snapshot scope, polygon holes, and preservation of all earlier case/progress behavior. Live Protected Planet smoke query returned 80 boundaries, an October 2026 source citation, and an explicit partial-results flag. All 20 hosted photo hashes and dimensions and source bounding boxes were independently verified; exporter regeneration is deterministic with a fixed timestamp.

Verification limit: native Computer Use was stopped by the user in the prior turn and was not resumed. DOM tests do not verify actual WebGL drawing or visual/mobile layout. The Natural Earth source image was visually inspected. deck.gl GlobeView is experimental; zoom is capped at 6 for regional overview. Production build emits the existing large mapping-bundle warning.
